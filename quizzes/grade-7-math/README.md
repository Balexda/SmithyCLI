# Grade 7 Math Quizzes

Five printable quizzes covering the core Grade 7 math strands. Each quiz is a
self-contained Markdown file with a name/date/score header, instructions, a formula
or vocabulary reference, 12 questions, and a full **answer key with reasoning** at the
bottom.

| # | Quiz | Topic | Items |
|---|------|-------|-------|
| 1 | [Coordinate Plane](01-coordinate-plane.md) | Ordered pairs, quadrants, plotting, distance, perimeter and area on a grid | 12 |
| 2 | [Geometry](02-geometry.md) | Complementary/supplementary/vertical angles, circle circumference and area, triangle and trapezoid area, scale drawings, cross-sections, Triangle Inequality | 12 |
| 3 | [Functions](03-functions.md) | Proportional relationships, constant of proportionality, tables, graphs, equations, function-machine rules | 12 |
| 4 | [Probability](04-probability.md) | Simple and compound events, sample spaces, complements, theoretical vs. experimental probability, predictions, dependent events | 12 |
| 5 | [Statistics](05-statistics.md) | Mean, median, mode, range, MAD, IQR, random vs. biased samples, population inferences, outliers | 12 |

## Using these quizzes

- **To print or hand out:** the answer key lives at the bottom of each file — delete
  that section (everything from the `## Answer Key` heading down) before distributing.
- **To self-check:** work the questions, then scroll to the key. Each row explains the
  reasoning, not just the final answer.
- **Suggested order:** the quizzes are independent and can be given in any order,
  though quiz 1 is a useful warm-up for the grid work that appears in quiz 2.

## Conventions

- Use **π ≈ 3.14** unless a question states otherwise.
- Probabilities are given as fractions in simplest form unless a decimal or percent is
  requested.
- Coordinates are written as `(x, y)`, x first.
