## Questions

**1.** Two angles are **complementary**. One measures 34°. Find the other.

_______________________________

**2.** Two angles are **supplementary**. One measures 118°. Find the other.

_______________________________

**3.** Two lines cross. One of the **vertical angles** measures 73°. What is the
measure of the angle vertical to it? Explain how you know.

_______________________________

**4.** A circle has a radius of 7 cm. Find its **circumference**.

_______________________________

**5.** A circle has a diameter of 10 in. Find its **area**.

_______________________________

**6.** Two angles of a triangle measure 45° and 62°. Find the third angle.

_______________________________

**7.** On a scale drawing, **1 cm represents 3 m**. A hallway is drawn 8 cm long.
How long is the actual hallway?

_______________________________

**8.** Find the area of a trapezoid with bases of 6 ft and 10 ft and a height of 5 ft.

_______________________________

**9.** A right rectangular prism is sliced by a plane **parallel to its base**.
What shape is the cross-section?

_______________________________

**10.** Can a triangle have side lengths of 4 cm, 5 cm, and 12 cm? Explain.

_______________________________

**11.** A triangle has a base of 9 m and a height of 6 m. Find its area.

_______________________________

**12.** Angle `ABD` and angle `DBC` are **adjacent** angles that together form a
straight line. If angle `ABD` = 47°, find angle `DBC`.

_______________________________

---

## Answer Key

| # | Answer | Reasoning |
|---|--------|-----------|
| 1 | **56°** | Complementary angles sum to 90°: 90 − 34 = 56 |
| 2 | **62°** | Supplementary angles sum to 180°: 180 − 118 = 62 |
| 3 | **73°** | Vertical angles are formed by two intersecting lines and are always congruent |
| 4 | **43.96 cm** | C = 2πr = 2(3.14)(7) = 43.96 |
| 5 | **78.5 in²** | d = 10 so r = 5; A = πr² = 3.14(25) = 78.5 |
| 6 | **73°** | 180 − 45 − 62 = 73 |
| 7 | **24 m** | 8 cm × 3 m per cm = 24 m |
| 8 | **40 ft²** | A = ½(6 + 10)(5) = ½(16)(5) = 40 |
| 9 | A **rectangle** congruent to the base | A slice parallel to the base always matches the base's shape and size |
| 10 | **No** | Triangle Inequality: 4 + 5 = 9, which is less than 12, so the two shorter sides cannot meet |
| 11 | **27 m²** | A = ½bh = ½(9)(6) = 27 |
| 12 | **133°** | Angles forming a straight line are supplementary: 180 − 47 = 133 |
