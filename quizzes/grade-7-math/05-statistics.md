### Vocabulary reference

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Mean** | Add all values, divide by how many there are |
| **Median** | The middle value once the data is ordered |
| **Mode** | The value that appears most often |
| **Range** | Greatest value − least value |
| **MAD** | Mean Absolute Deviation — the average distance of each value from the mean |
| **IQR** | Interquartile Range — Q3 − Q1 |

## Answer Key

| # | Answer | Reasoning |
|---|--------|-----------|
| 1 | **14** | (12 + 15 + 9 + 20 + 14) = 70; 70 ÷ 5 = 14 |
| 2 | **6.5** | Ordered: 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10. With 6 values, average the middle two: (6 + 7) ÷ 2 = 6.5 |
| 3 | **16** | 31 − 15 = 16 |
| 4 | **4** | 4 appears three times; every other value appears once |
| 5 | **2** | Mean = 20 ÷ 4 = 5. Distances from 5: 3, 1, 1, 3. Sum = 8; 8 ÷ 4 = 2 |
| 6 | **(a)** | A random sample from the full student list represents the whole population. The cooking club is not random — those students may have unusual food preferences |
| 7 | (a) **80%**  (b) about **480 students** | 40 ÷ 50 = 0.80 = 80%; 0.80 × 600 = 480 |
| 8 | Both teams have the **same typical score (82)**, but **Team A was more consistent**. A MAD of 3 means scores sat about 3 points from the mean, while Team B's MAD of 9 means scores were spread much farther apart | A smaller MAD means less variability |
| 9 | A sample is **biased** when it is not representative of the whole population, so some groups are over- or under-represented. Example: surveying only students in the band about the school's favorite music class | — |
| 10 | **8** | Lower half 4, 6, 8, 10 → Q1 = 7. Upper half 12, 14, 16, 18 → Q3 = 15. IQR = 15 − 7 = 8 |
| 11 | The sample is **biased**. People at a skate park are far more likely than the general population to favor skateboarding, so the sample does not represent the whole town | A valid conclusion needs a random sample of townspeople |
| 12 | (a) **67** (thousand)  (b) **35** (thousand)  (c) The **median** | The mean is 335 ÷ 5 = 67, pulled far upward by the single $200K outlier. Four of the five employees earn less than the mean, so the median of 35 better describes a typical salary |
